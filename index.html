WHAT IS SBEF?
Standby Earth Fault relay sits on the LV 415V side. It detects high-impedance earth faults that the HV earth fault relay cannot see. Very sensitive — typically 5% of FLC. Operates as backup if main earth fault relay fails.

PART A — HIGH SET INSTANTANEOUS I>> and Ie>> (ANSI 50)
Set at 15× In for overcurrent and 4× In for earth fault. Trips in milliseconds — no IDMT timing. Used for heavy faults very close to the transformer where damage would occur within one cycle. Pickup tolerance check: relay must pick up within ±5% of setting.

PART B — THERMAL / LOCKOUT ELEMENT I>>> and Ie>>> (ANSI 49)
Set at 0.78× In. Operating time 180 seconds. This is NOT instantaneous — it is a definite time element. It detects sustained overload conditions that would cause thermal damage to the transformer winding over time. 180 seconds allows temporary overloads to ride through without tripping.

WHY 180 SECONDS?
Transformer windings have thermal mass — they take time to heat up. A brief overload for a few seconds causes no damage. Sustained overload for 3 minutes (180s) causes insulation degradation and eventual failure. The 180s setting matches the transformer thermal damage curve at this overload level.

WHY 0.78× In SETTING?
This is deliberately set BELOW full load current (below 1× In). It monitors the transformer continuously and trips on sustained overcurrent before thermal damage accumulates. Note: Earth fault element I>>> not applicable (shown as dash in your test report — correct for this protection scheme).
